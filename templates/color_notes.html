Notes on your home screen

A small Android app adds a Color Notes widget to your home screen. It shows your pinned notes (or your latest ones) in their colours, lets you tick checklist items, and opens a note when you tap it.

  1. Install the widget app on your Android phone.
    Download the app (APK)
    Android asks once to allow installing apps from Chrome or Files. It isn't from the Play Store, so Play Protect may ask you to confirm; choose Install anyway.
  2. Connect this account, on the same phone.
    If the app did not open, copy these into it:
  3. Add the widget: in the app, tap Add widget to home screen. Or long-press an empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → DailyPlanner Notes.

Notes on this phone

Your notes are saved in this phone's storage, so Color Notes opens and saves without internet. Changes made offline are sent to your account when you're back online, and then appear on your other devices.

To keep a copy you can open anywhere, or move notes to another phone, save a backup file to your phone's Downloads.
